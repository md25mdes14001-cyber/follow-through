Seva Circle and Trust Receipt

A clickable prototype of two service ideas from the Aham Brahmasmi project, rebuilt for the Follow-Through thesis. It shows how the service would feel. It does not show results.

What is real here

Nothing in this demo is field data. Every weight, date, head-count and destination is sample data written for the demo. No circle, collector, aggregator or recycler has used it.

What it is built to test (planned)

  • Trust Receipt: does telling a group where its waste went raise segregation quality and repeat participation?
  • Seva Circle: does a 10-minute weekly routine keep people taking part after week one?
  • Framing: does a cultural (seva) frame beat a secular ownership frame? Both are offered; neither is required.

Deliberately left out

Scripture quotations (not yet checked by a scholar), points and leaderboards, app-store badges and any impact claim above rung 3 of the Claim Ladder. The first field version runs on paper cards and WhatsApp; this screen flow is the specification for that.

Keyboard: Tab moves between controls; Enter or Space activates them.
