03 · The proposed projectPlanned

The pilot adds one weekly routine, a receipt and a claim rule, then tests them against matched groups for eight weeks

What happens

What is measured

What can be claimed

Study design

  • Where: a project site with its workmen colony, nearby schools and women's groups; a campus sprint first (Dec–Jan) to rehearse the method.
  • Changes: the weekly Trust Receipt, yes or no (H1). Identity framing is isolated on campus (H2).
  • Measures: repeat participation, contamination % by weight, kg per contributor per week, receiver acceptance, cost per kg, collector safety.
  • Held steady: service, schedule, collector and receiver; no household fees.

Learning

Why follow-through decays after week one, and which of service, feedback or framing holds it, measured in an Indian field setting.

Innovation

Per-batch feedback to contributors plus a claim rule: none of the 19 models reviewed states either. What is new is the combination.

Design contribution

A service blueprint with an evidence lane, the Trust Receipt format, the Claim Ladder and a reusable field kit.

SOURCES Thesis dossier (research design, hypotheses H1–H2, measures); models and strategies review, 19 programmes, pages opened 16 Nov 2026 ("none states" covers only the pages reviewed). Phase length: 8 measured weeks inside an 8–10-week site phase.

Low-tech parts

  • A hanging scale, two bags per group, paper or WhatsApp receipts.
  • Ten minutes inside a routine people already have.
  • No app to install; a digital version only after the manual service works.

Works with what exists

  • Local collectors are paid, never bypassed; saleable material stays with local buyers.
  • The receiver for low-value plastic is confirmed in week one; ecobricks are the stated fallback.
  • Cost per kg is reported every week, with its parts.

Risk and fallback

  • Ethics approval and written site permission come first.
  • If a site is not confirmed at the first gate, the campus becomes the main site.
  • Group-level data only; nothing reaches a supervisor.

Language and literacy

  • Hindi and English throughout.
  • Picture cards for the two streams.
  • Receipts read aloud at the weekly handover, or sent as a voice note.

Screens and print

  • Digital screens meet WCAG 2.2 level AA (text contrast at least 4.5:1).
  • Large type on paper receipts; nothing depends on colour alone.

Dignity and choice

  • A cultural frame is optional and always sits beside a secular one.
  • No data from children; anyone can stop at any time.
  • Collectors are named and thanked, and paid for their time.

05 · Value and the askValue models: conceptPlanned

Once the pilot proves it, the service can pay its way in three ways. To get there it needs one site and one mentor.

No price is assumed here: prices follow the pilot's measured cost per kg. Select a model

What I ask for

  • A field partner: 8–14 weeks of site access, one named contact, ten minutes a week inside an existing routine, help with weekly pickup and an introduction to a receiver.
  • An industry or faculty mentor: about two hours a fortnight, and a check of the Claim Ladder against real BRSR, GRI or CSR reporting.
  • Permission to publish anonymised, group-level results.

What the partner gets

A tested collection-and-feedback service, a cost-per-kg estimate, a Claim Ladder fitted to its reporting, a reusable blueprint and kit, and an honest account of what did not work.
