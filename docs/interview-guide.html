Interview guide: sachet waste and public-space behaviour (revised)

Status: PLANNED instrument. Not yet used. Replaces the six guides in Smokeless Tobacco/01_Primary_Research/B_Interviews/ for field use. Use only with ethics approval, the consent note (CONSENT_NOTE_EN_HI.md) and the sampling plan (SAMPLING_PLAN.md). Questions are in the spoken Hindi of the original guides (Roman script), with English meaning. A native speaker should check the wording before the pilot interview.

Rules for every interview

  1. Read the consent script first. No covert interviewing: the original instruction "Do NOT show this paper … memorize key questions" is withdrawn.
  2. Never ask anyone to confirm an offence: no questions about selling banned products, selling to children, paying police, or their own spitting. Ask about the place and "people around here" instead.
  3. Use open questions. No yes/no questions that tell the respondent the answer. One idea per question.
  4. Stop if the respondent is under 18, distressed, or being watched by an employer or supervisor.
  5. Record a participant code, never a name. Do not record shop names, beat numbers or exact locations.
  6. 20–30 minutes. Thank the respondent. Pay cleaners and waste pickers for their time at the agreed rate.

Common opening (all respondents)

  • "Aap yahan kitne samay se kaam kar rahe hain / aate hain?" (How long have you worked here / come here?)
  • "Is jagah par safaai aur kachre ke baare mein aapko sabse pehle kya dikhta hai?" (What do you notice first about cleanliness and waste here?)

Module A: Adult consumers (Version C consent)

# Ask Replaces (original) Why changed
A1 "Aap kaun-kaun si cheezein lete hain — gutkha, khaini, bidi, cigarette ya kuch aur?" "Aap kya khaate / peete hain? Kitne time se?" Split a double question
A2 "Ise lete hue kitne saal hue?" same Split
A3 "Din mein aap ise kab-kab lete hain? Koi khaas samay ya mauka?" "Kab zyada lagti hai — khaana khane ke baad, stress mein…?" Removed leading options
A4 "Aapke hisaab se iska sehat par koi asar padta hai? Kaisa asar?" "Kya aapko pata hai ki yeh sehat ke liye theek nahi hai?" Was leading
A5 "Khaali pouch ka aap aam taur par kya karte hain?" "Pouch / wrapper kahaan phenkte hain?" Presumed littering
A6 "Aapke aas-paas log khaane ke baad aam taur par kahan thookte hain?" "Khaane ke baad thokte kahan hain — road pe, nali mein…?" Third person; no self-incrimination
A7 "Sadak par thookne ke baare mein aapki kya raay hai?" "Kya aapko lagta hai road pe thookna galat hai?" Was moralising yes/no
A8 "Agar yeh milna mushkil ya mehenga ho jaaye, toh aap kya karenge?" "Agar yeh band ho jaaye…" Gutkha is already banned in MP
A9 "Kya aapne kabhi chhodne ki koshish ki?" → only if yes or unsure: "Chhodne mein sabse mushkil kya lagta hai?" "Kya kabhi chhodni ki koshish ki?" / "Koi cheez hai jo aapko rokti hai chhodne se?" Typo; did not presume a wish to quit
A10 "Agar paas mein pouch daalne ki jagah ho, toh kya badlega?" (new) The design question for the thesis

At the end, offer the verified national quitline number, without pressure.

Module B: Shopkeepers and paan vendors (Version G consent; highest legal risk)

# Ask Replaces Why
B1 "Tambaaku aur paan masala ki maang pichhle kuch saalon mein kaise badli hai?" "Ek din mein kitne packet gutkha bik jaate hain?" Volume of a banned product is incriminating
B2 "Dukaan ke aas-paas khaali pouch aur thook ka kya hota hai? Kaun saaf karta hai?" "Aapko lagta hai yeh problem hai?" Was leading; now about waste
B3 "Kya dukaan ke paas koi dabba ya thookdaan rakha hai? Log use karte hain?" (new) Infrastructure question
B4 "Tambaaku bechne ke baare mein aapko kaun-kaun se niyam pata hain? Kahan se pata chala?" "Kya aapko COTPA ke baare mein pata hai — school ke 100 meter…?" Knowledge test, wrong unit (the law says 100 yards)
B5 "Is ilaake mein kam umar ke log tambaaku kahan se le lete hain, aapke hisaab se?" "Kya bacche bhi kharidne aate hain?" / "Kya aap unhe bechte hain?" Third person; the original asked for a confession
B6 "Agar tambaaku waali cheezon par aur sakhti ho, toh aapke kaam par kya asar padega?" "Agar gutkha band ho jaye…" Already banned
— Deleted: police visits (could surface bribery), loose sales, ban orders "kya hua phir". Legal harm

Module C: Municipal cleaners and sanitation workers (interview off shift, away from the supervisor)

# Ask Replaces Why
C1 "Safaai mein sabse zyada kaun si cheezein milti hain?" "…gutkha pouch, cigarette bidi, plastic?" Primed gutkha
C2 "Thook ke daag kitni baar saaf karne padte hain?" "Thoone ke daag kitne…? Kaise saaf karte hain?" Split; typo
C3 "Unhe kaise saaf karte hain — kya saaman milta hai? Dastaane milte hain?" same Split; adds PPE
C4 "Is kaam mein sabse mushkil hissa kaunsa hai?" "Aapko sabse zyada gussa kab aata hai?" Presumed anger
C5 (optional, skip if uncomfortable) "Kya kabhi aisa hua ki kisi ne aapke saamne hi thook diya? Tab kya hua?" "…aur aap kuch bol paaye?" Double question; risk of distress
C6 "Agar log pouch alag rakhein, toh aapke kaam mein kya farak padega?" (new) Links to the thesis service

Module D: Informal waste pickers and kabadiwalas

# Ask Replaces Why
D1 "Kya koi kabaadi gutkha ke khaali pouch kharidta hai? Kis daam par?" "Gutkha ka pouch recycle hota hai?" Hindi and English asked different things
D2 "Kaun si cheezein uthana faayde ka hai aur kaun si nahi? Kyun?" (new) Core thesis question: what is worth collecting
D3 "Aapka kaam aasaan karne ke liye kya badalna chahiye?" "Agar ek cheez band ho jaaye…?" Led towards "ban"
— Deleted: own earnings in detail; own tobacco use. Sensitive and not needed

Module E: Traffic police (with written permission from the station)

# Ask Replaces
E1 "Duty ke dauraan thookne ki ghatnaayein kitni baar dikhti hain — roz, kabhi-kabhi, ya kam?" "Kitne logon ko aap … thookte ya gutkha khaate dekhte hain?"
E2 "Is niyam ko laagu karne mein kya-kya mushkilein aati hain?" "Kya aapko lagta hai yeh niyam enforce ho sakta hai?"
E3 "Aapke hisaab se is samasya ki sabse badi wajah kya hai?" "…log, niyam, ya system?" (forced choice)
— Deleted: "orders from above"; never having fined anyone; alcohol (out of scope).

Module F: Doctors and dentists (in English or Hindi)

# Ask Replaces
F1 "Which tobacco-related conditions do you see most often?" "…oral cancer, COPD, hypertension, dental issues?" (leading list)
F2 "How often do you refer patients to a quitline or cessation centre, and what happens after referral?" "Is there a National Tobacco Cessation helpline? Do patients actually use it?"
F3 "In your patients, what keeps people using tobacco?" (probe each factor separately) "What role does addiction versus habit versus social pressure play?"
F4 "Which approaches worked less well than expected? What explains that?" "What approaches have consistently failed and why?"
F5 "May I quote you by role, for example 'a dentist in a district hospital', or not at all?" (new)

Closing (all respondents)

  • "Is baare mein aur kuch batana chahenge jo maine nahi poocha?" (Anything else I did not ask?)
  • Thank, pay where agreed, and repeat how to withdraw.

Fixes to the other instruments (apply before use)

  • Screener: age screen first (stop if under 18); fix the stop rule (stop if S1 = none or S2 = no); separate "gutkha" from non-tobacco "pan masala"; make ranges non-overlapping (<1 / 1–<3 / 3–<10 / ≥10); fully labelled five-point scales; "Prefer not to say" on every sensitive item; Hindi translation with back-translation.
  • Observation template: photograph places and litter, never people; never photograph minors; blur faces and shop signboards before storage; paraphrase overheard speech without identifiers; count events per fixed 15-minute interval instead of "low/medium/high"; second observer on a sample of sessions.
  • Waste audit template: fixed 10 m × 10 m plot only (no "full area" option); counts, not estimates; hours since last sweep; a "label shows tobacco / no tobacco / unreadable" field; gloves, tongs and disposal steps.
  • Insight cards: stamp both example cards "FICTIONAL EXAMPLE — NOT DATA"; remove the quote "Sab marna hi hai ek din", the "78%" figure and the ticked "pattern across multiple sources" box.
