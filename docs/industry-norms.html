Industry norms and what partners expect

Status: desk research DONE, 16 November 2026. Used by the five-slide proposal (04_Presentable/proposal/). Every row names the page opened. No company is named; partners are described by role.

If you read only this

A company that hosts or funds a waste pilot in India now reports waste against named rules: SEBI's BRSR Core (with reasonable assurance phased in to the top 1,000 listed companies by FY 2026–27), GRI 306 for global reporting, CSR law for the spend, plastic-packaging EPR for brand owners and, from 1 April 2026, the new solid-waste rules for bulk generators. All of them ask for weights by category and evidence of where waste went. That is exactly what the Trust Receipt and the Claim Ladder produce at batch level. The proposal is built to slot into these reports, not to add a new one.

1. Norms the pilot must fit

Norm What it asks (from the page opened) Where the pilot fits Source opened Status
BRSR Core, SEBI circular SEBI/HO/CFD/CFD-SEC-2/P/CIR/2023/122, 12 Jul 2023 Principle 6, Essential Indicator 9: total waste generated by category (plastic, e-waste, bio-medical, C&D, battery, radioactive, other hazardous, other non-hazardous), waste recovered through recycling, re-use or other recovery, and waste disposed by method. Reasonable assurance of BRSR Core on a glide path: top 150 listed entities (FY 2023–24), 250 (2024–25), 500 (2025–26), 1,000 (2026–27). The assurance approach notes disclosure "if certificates from vendors have been relied upon for assurance of KPIs on waste management". Each batch carries a weight, a receiver's slip and a Claim Ladder rung: a vendor-evidence trail an assurer can follow. Copy of the circular (52 pp) at ca2013.com, read 16 Nov 2026; sebi.gov.in page showed the title only. Later SEBI relaxations (value-chain items) not opened. verified (circular text)
GRI 306: Waste 2020 Disclosures 306-1 (waste generation and significant impacts), 306-2 (management of impacts), 306-3 (waste generated, metric tons by composition), 306-4 (waste diverted from disposal), 306-5 (waste directed to disposal). 306-2 guidance names data processes such as weighbridge measurement and external validation, and third parties "formally or informally involved" in handling waste. Ladder rungs 3–5 map to 306-4 only when a recovery record exists; otherwise the weight stays in 306-3. Informal collectors are named as third parties. globalreporting.org PDF (pdf.ashx?id=12521), 30 pp, read 16 Nov 2026 verified
CSR, Companies Act 2013, s.135 and Schedule VII A company with net worth ₹500 crore+, turnover ₹1,000 crore+ or net profit ₹5 crore+ spends at least 2% of average net profit on CSR; Schedule VII includes "ensuring environmental sustainability, ecological balance…" and conservation of natural resources. The pilot is a CSR-eligible environmental activity; its outcomes are stated at their evidenced rung. ies.gov.in Arthapedia (Indian Economic Service), read 16 Nov 2026. MCA's own page returned 403. verified (government summary)
Plastic-packaging EPR, PWM (Amendment) Rules 2022, G.S.R. 133(E), 16 Feb 2022 Producers, importers and brand owners register on a central CPCB portal and meet EPR targets with certificates. CSE (2024) found certificates generated by wrongful means were traded. A documented chain of custody for low-value plastic routed to an EPR processor or co-processor. 02_Research/sources/statistics_verified.md §11; FACT_SHEET.md row 29–30 verified
Solid Waste Management Rules 2026, in force 1 Apr 2026 Segregation into four streams (wet, dry, sanitary, special care). Bulk waste generators (any one of: 20,000 m² floor area, 40,000 L water a day, 100 kg solid waste a day) register and report on a CPCB portal and take Extended Bulk Waste Generator Responsibility certificates from the local body. A weekly weigh-in gives a site, campus or township the dry-stream figures it now has to report. Legal500 / Lexplosion summary, read 16 Nov 2026. Gazette text not opened. partly verified: details to be checked against the gazette
WCAG 2.2, W3C Recommendation (current version dated 12 Dec 2024) Text contrast at least 4.5:1 at level AA (1.4.3); 3:1 for large text. Every digital screen of the receipt and kit meets AA. w3.org/TR/WCAG22, read 16 Nov 2026 verified

Not used, because the page could not be opened: ISO 59020:2024 (circularity measurement; iso.org returned 403) and ISO 14001. Cite them only after reading them.

Infrastructure or construction contractor (site CSR and EHS) A CSR-eligible activity with low disruption to work; community goodwill around the site; figures that survive assurance; no new liability for workers or children Ten minutes inside an existing routine; paid local collectors; group-level data only; claims stopped at the evidenced rung
